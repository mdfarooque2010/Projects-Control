Inside the Analytics Suite

  • Earned Value Management: PV, EV, SV, SPI, budget-weighted progress.
  • Calendar-Exact Float: Total Float calculated against each activity's real P6 calendar.
  • Integrated Excel Templates: Built-in templates for Engineering Deliverables (MDR & weight-vs-progress), RFIs, Material Procurement, and GIS Linear Progress Mapping (using Latitude & Longitude for alignment progress).
  • Automated Delay Report (Word & PDF): Variance calculation and narrative delay reporting generated by schedule comparison.
  • DCMA 14-Point Assessment: Schedule quality checks with offender activity lists.
  • Monte Carlo Risk: P10 / P50 / P90 completion date forecasting.
  • Automated Risk Register: 5×5 probability-impact matrix visualization.
  • S-Curves & Histograms: Manpower, resource loading, delay distribution, look-ahead.
  • Combined Portfolio Mode: Merge several package schedules into a single project view.
  • Period Comparison: Week-over-week tracking to see exactly what moved.
  • Field Management Logs: Templates to record HSE incidents, NCRs, SORs, and weather delays.
