Sheet 05 — Why offline

Your schedule never leaves your machine

Every tool in this set reads a standard P6 export or database locally. No project data is uploaded anywhere to generate a report, audit a baseline, or perform delay analysis — a deliberate choice for an industry where schedules are confidential.
