Core Capabilities

Enterprise Construction Document Intelligence

  • Automated PDF Data Extraction: Reads RFI number, revision, discipline, chainage/location, submission date, and engineer's stamped approval status.
  • Inbuilt Standard Template Generator (.xlsx): Download a pre-configured, formula-ready RFI register directly inside the tool with Civil, Electrical, Mechanical, ICT, and HSE sheets.
  • Reviewer & Signatory KPIs (Col K): Turnaround duration, SLA compliance %, approval rates, and workload tracking conforming to international standards (FIDIC / CMAA / AIA).
  • Multi-Package Tested: Built for multi-package infrastructure programmes across diverse contractor forms (Civil, Structural, MEP, and Process).
