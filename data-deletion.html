Sheet DD-01 — Data deletion

Request deletion of your data

Applies to Progress Schedulytics for Android and for Windows, published by Mohammed Farooque.
Last updated 11 August 2026.

Your project data is never uploaded. Primavera P6 schedules, the reports generated from them, and any Excel template you supply are read and processed entirely on your own device. They are never transmitted to us, so there is nothing on our side to delete.

1. How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to md.farooque@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion request — Progress Schedulytics”.
  2. Include the email address you used at purchase and, if you have it, your licence key. We need one of these to locate the record.
  3. Say whether you want your licence deactivated, your purchase record erased, or both.
  4. We confirm by reply and complete the deletion within 30 days.

There is no account to close and no password to reset — the app has no sign-in.

2. To delete everything held on your device — uninstall the app

The licence file, the trial record and every report you generated are stored in the app's own private storage. Uninstalling Progress Schedulytics (or using Settings → Apps → Progress Schedulytics → Storage → Clear data) removes all of it immediately. No request to us is required.

3. What data exists, and what happens to it

DataWhere it is heldDeletion
P6 schedules, generated dashboards, Excel, PowerPoint and Word/PDF reports, Excel templates Your device only — never transmitted Deleted when you uninstall or clear app data. We never hold a copy.
Licence file and free-trial record The app's private storage on your device Deleted when you uninstall or clear app data.
Device identifier (Android ID) and licence key, sent once when you activate Transmitted over HTTPS to our activation service, which is stateless and stores nothing Nothing is retained, so there is nothing to delete.
Purchase record — name, email, licence key Held by our payment processor, Gumroad Erased on request. See the retention note below.

4. What may be kept, and for how long

Where a purchase has been made, tax and accounting law may require us to retain a minimal transaction record — the invoice amount, date and country — for up to seven years. Where that applies we erase or anonymise your name, email address and licence key, and keep only what the law requires. Nothing retained for this purpose can be used to identify you as a user of the app.

5. Effect of deletion

Deleting your purchase record ends your licence. The app continues to work, but reverts to trial behaviour and its outputs carry a trial watermark again. This cannot be undone, and re-activating later requires a new purchase.
