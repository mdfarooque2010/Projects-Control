Frequently Asked Questions about Primavera P6 Viewing

Do I need Oracle database software or Primavera P6 to use this viewer?
No. Schedulytics is 100% standalone. It parses raw .xer and .xml files using its own internal CPM calculation engine. You do not need Oracle database, SQLite, Java, or Primavera P6 installed.

Can I view P6 schedules on my Android smartphone on the jobsite?
Yes. Every Progress Schedulytics license includes dual-device activation: 1 Windows desktop PC and 1 Android phone, allowing planning engineers to check WBS progress and critical paths right from the jobsite.
